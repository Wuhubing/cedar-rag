Wuhubing

This project helped me unpack how RAG decouples document curation, retrieval, and response generation. Queries and source snippets are converted into searchable representations, and the retrieved segments feed into a new chat turn to generate cited responses grounded on individual chunks.

Embedding-only retrieval revealed a clear shortcoming. On Q1, a top‑k of 2 pulled A2 and T1 while overlooking A1’s opening-hour details, and raising k to 3 still failed to surface A1. Q4 further demonstrated that positive similarity alone does not mean a passage can answer the question. Semantic similarity merely signals thematic overlap; every retrieved excerpt must contain factual backing for the claim being made.

I plan to evaluate hybrid retrieval that merges keyword matching with embedding ordering. I will check whether this method recovers essential sources without introducing too much extraneous context. For datasets with useful figures, I also want to investigate image embeddings and cross-modal search.
